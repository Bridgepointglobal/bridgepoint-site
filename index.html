Questions

Before you book a call.

01Who do you work with, and who is it not for?

Health and service businesses, clinicians, founders and experts who want AI, technology and visibility done properly. It is not for anyone looking for a quick template or a cheap shortcut.

02What happens on the first call?

Thirty minutes, no pitch. We listen, ask the questions that matter, and you leave with a clearer next step whether or not we work together.

03Who does the work?

Every engagement is led by Dr Suzanne, with specialist support behind her. You always know who is accountable.

04How long until we see results?

It depends on the scope and what you need. We agree a realistic timeline with you before any work starts.

05How does pricing work?

Every project is scoped after the first call. You get a clear proposal setting out what is included and what it costs before you commit to anything.

06Is our data safe?

Yes. Everything we build is secure by design and GDPR compliant, and we tell you clearly wherever AI is involved.

07Do you work outside Moldova?

Yes. We work worldwide, in English and Romanian.

08How are you different from an agency or a freelancer?

A doctor and engineer leads every engagement, so the work is clinically grounded and technically sound, not just marketing.
